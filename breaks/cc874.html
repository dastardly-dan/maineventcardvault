How letters get assigned

Colfax's break page doesn't spell out its letter rules, so these are the usual conventions. Check with Colfax on anything borderline.

First letter of the printed name

  • The spot goes by the first letter of the name printed on the card.
  • A leading "The" doesn't count: The Rock is R, The Miz is M, The Maker is M, The Thing is T.

Two-letter spots split on the second letter

  • Shohei is SH, so he's in SA-SH.
  • Spider-Man is SP, so SI-SZ.
  • Doctor Doom is DO, so DF-DZ. Derrick Henry is DE, so DA-DE.

Gray areas to ask about

  • Nicknames: a card printed "The American Nightmare" would land in A, while "Cody Rhodes" lands in C.
  • Dual and multi-subject cards, and Pokémon cards with two names (Darkrai & Cresselia).
  • Non-player cards like Trainer and item cards in the Pokémon pack.

How solid is this list?

  • Samurai Japan, Marvel Neon and Wild Card SPLAT come from their base or published checklists, so those are the most complete.
  • The NFL, WWE and NBA sections list well-known names, not every card number or parallel.
  • The Pokémon names are candidates. Nobody has confirmed what's in this exact booster, so treat that column loosely.
  • "None listed" means no named base subject turned up for that box and letter. An insert or dual card could still land there.
  • Every name was checked against its spot's letter range, and 8 names from the original list were moved to the right spot.
