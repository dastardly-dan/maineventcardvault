Spot builder

Pick the checklists in your break, set how many spots you want, and it splits the letters into the most even spots it can. Heavy letters get cut into two-letter ranges (SA-SH, SI-SZ) or Men and Women when that evens things out.

1. Boxes in the break

Add "| W" for women and "| auto" for autograph names. Pasted names count as one box.

2. Spots

Best balance mixes light letters together (like Q/V/Z). A to Z keeps neighbors together (like E-F).
Simple keeps whole letters wherever it can. Most even cuts more letters to get every spot closer to the same size.

3. Letter rule

A leading "The" is always skipped, so The Rock is R.

4. What counts more

Points per appearance, per box. A name in the base set and an auto set scores base plus auto.
Use this for the names buyers actually chase, so their letters get treated as the heavy hitters they are. One huge star can't be split, so their spot may stay a bit heavier.

How it works

1. Score every name

  • Each name earns points for every box it can come out of: base, autograph, insert, multi-person card.
  • Two boxes of the same product count twice.
  • Star multipliers scale a name up, so a Cena letter outweighs a letter full of NXT rookies.

2. Split the heavy letters

  • A fair share is the total score divided by your spot count.
  • Any letter above about 1.3 fair shares gets cut, either by second letter (SA-SH, SI-SZ) or into Men and Women, whichever evens it out better.
  • It keeps cutting until there are enough pieces to fill every spot.

3. Pack the spots

  • Best balance: biggest pieces go first into the lightest spot, then it swaps pieces between the heaviest and lightest spots until nothing improves.
  • A to Z order: finds the cut points along the alphabet that land closest to a fair share each.

4. Price it (optional)

  • Enter what you need the break to bring in, and each spot is priced by its share of the total score, rounded to the dollar.
  • Treat it as a starting point. Hype, rookies and case hits move real prices.

The score is only as good as the checklist and your star list. Breakers still make the final call on nicknames, multi-person cards and anything the checklist misses.
