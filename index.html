How it works

Condition

Every card is ungraded and near mint or better unless the listing says otherwise. Photos are of the actual card you receive — no stock images.

Shipping

$5.99 flat, USPS Ground Advantage — $0.30 each additional card

Packaging

Penny sleeve and a 35pt toploader or larger, sized to the card. A card that arrives to me already in a one-touch — or that warrants one on its own value — ships back out in that one-touch instead. Then team bag and rigid chipboard in a bubble mailer. Ships within two business days.

Buying

Every purchase goes through eBay, so you get their buyer protection and tracked delivery. Offers welcome on most listings.
